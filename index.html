About BHKFPCL

Collective Strength for Sustainable Farming in Purba Medinipur

Baba Hatnagar Krishikranti Farmers Producer Company Limited (BHKFPCL) is a registered producer company (CIN: U01139WB2023PTC263793) headquartered at Rasan, Egra, Purba Medinipur, West Bengal. Established in 2023, the organization brings smallholder and marginal farmers onto a unified collective platform.

Our mission is to eliminate middlemen barriers through fair paddy procurement, build farmer capacity via practical training programs, and expand into affordable fertilizer, pesticide, and modern agricultural machinery distribution.
